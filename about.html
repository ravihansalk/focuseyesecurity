Our Story

Built on a Simple Idea: Security Should Be Smarter

FocusEye Security was founded to close the gap between complex enterprise surveillance systems and the affordable, easy-to-install kits available to everyday businesses and homeowners. What started as a small team of installers sourcing quality components has grown into a full-range supplier of CCTV, access control and networked security solutions across New Zealand.

Today, we work with installers, trade professionals, businesses and homeowners to deliver security systems that are reliable, scalable and genuinely easy to use — backed by local support that actually picks up the phone.

Our Mission

"Smarter Security. Safer Tomorrow."

We believe every business and family deserves access to intelligent, reliable security — without the complexity or the enterprise price tag. That mission drives every product we stock and every kit we design.
