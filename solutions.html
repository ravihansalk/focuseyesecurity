Shops, cafes and restaurants deal with high foot traffic, staff turnover and stock loss all at once. We combine CCTV, access control and analytics so you can protect stock and staff without slowing down service.

  • HD cameras covering entry points, tills and stockrooms
  • Loss-prevention and till-area monitoring
  • Queue and foot-traffic analytics
  • After-hours access control for staff
  • Cloud footage for dispute resolution and insurance claims
