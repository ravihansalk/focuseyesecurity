Site Survey & Design

Every property is different, so we start with a proper site assessment before recommending any equipment. Our technicians walk your site in person (or via video call for smaller jobs) to understand your risk areas, blind spots and existing infrastructure before designing a system that actually fits — not a generic package.

  • On-site walkthrough with a qualified technician
  • Risk and blind-spot assessment (entry points, perimeters, high-value areas)
  • Camera placement and coverage mapping
  • Cabling, power and network infrastructure review
  • Written proposal with product list, layout diagram and pricing

Preventive Maintenance

Security systems need upkeep like anything else. Our maintenance plans catch small issues — a loose cable, a failing drive, outdated firmware — before they become blind spots.

  • Scheduled health checks (quarterly or bi-annual)
  • Firmware and software updates
  • Storage (HDD) health monitoring and replacement
  • Lens cleaning and housing inspection
  • Priority callout response for existing customers
