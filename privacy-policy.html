Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

FocusEye Security ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Information we collect

We only collect personal information that you choose to give us, for example when you:

  • send an enquiry through the contact form on this website (your name, phone number, email address, the service you're interested in and your message)
  • email us, phone us or visit our head office
  • request a quote, book a site survey or apply for a trade account

This website does not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • to reply to your enquiry and prepare quotes
  • to arrange site surveys, installations, maintenance and support
  • to manage trade accounts, orders, invoices and warranty claims
  • to meet our legal and accounting obligations

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Third-party services

This website uses a small number of third-party services that may receive technical information such as your IP address:

  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to our email inbox
  • Google Maps displays the map of our head office
  • Google Fonts and unpkg supply the fonts and icons used on this site
  • Microsoft Azure hosts this website

Some of these providers may store data outside New Zealand. Each has its own privacy policy.

CCTV footage

When we install, service or remotely monitor a CCTV system, the footage belongs to the system owner. We only access footage when you ask us to, for example for support, maintenance or an agreed monitoring service. System owners are responsible for using CCTV in line with the Privacy Act and the Privacy Commissioner's CCTV guidance.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, and we keep it only for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law.

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. Email info@focuseye.co.nz, call 09 972 4227, or write to us at Unit 8/23 Springs Road, East Tamaki, Auckland.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
